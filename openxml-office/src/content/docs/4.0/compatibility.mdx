openxml-office v4.x is built on a single Rust core that is exposed to other
languages through a Rust FFI layer. The Rust crate talks to the core directly,
while every other language ships a thin wrapper around the FFI layer. The
generated documents target **Microsoft Office 2007 and newer**.
